# Verge Five SEO/AEO Traction Plan

## Positioning

Verge Five should be framed as the business credit readiness and approval-preparation system built from the ideas in "The Hidden Gatekeepers of Business Approval." The book explains the problem: automated underwriting, vendor checks, lender systems, and public records can judge a business before a person ever reviews it. Verge Five gives owners the step-by-step buildout that makes the business profile cleaner, more consistent, and easier to verify before applications.

## Conversion Path

1. Read a direct answer or guide.
2. Run the free business visibility scan.
3. Preview the platform on What's Inside or the test drive.
4. Start monthly access or annual access.

## Search Topics To Own

- Business credit readiness.
- Business visibility scan.
- NAP consistency for business credit.
- Automated underwriting for business credit.
- Business phone, address, website, and domain email setup.
- Vendor credit readiness.
- Net 30 vendor application timing.
- Business bank rating and bank relationship readiness.
- Business credit application denials.
