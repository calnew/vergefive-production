# Verge Five Project Instructions

This workspace is for the Verge Five business-credit education platform.

## Product Context

Verge Five teaches users how to build business credit step-by-step. The experience must be clear enough for beginners with no business-credit background.

Primary journey:

1. Start Here
2. Business Identity
3. Legal Setup
4. Banking Foundation
5. Business Plan
6. Approval Readiness
7. Starter Vendor Credit
8. Credit Tools
9. Bank Funding Options
10. Final Readiness Summary

## Working Principles

- Prioritize clarity over marketing language.
- Explain jargon immediately.
- Keep the user aware of where they are, why the current step matters, and what to do next.
- Do not encourage vendor, credit card, or funding applications before readiness checks are complete.
- Use conversational, confident, beginner-friendly copy.
- Preserve member trust: reports, billing, auth, and progress language must be precise.
