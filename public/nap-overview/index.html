NAP overview

Name, address, and phone must match before applications.

NAP means the business Name, Address, and Phone. This is the identity rule underneath Module 1. Before vendors, banks, credit cards, or funding review the business, public-facing records should point to the same company, same commercial address, and same business phone number.

NameUse the exact legal business name or approved DBA everywhere.AddressUse one commercial-style address format across records.PhoneUse a real business number that can connect to listings.
What happens next: after this lesson, move into the phone and Business 411 section. Then the address, website, and domain email sections complete the full identity stack.
