Why this matters

A Low 5 bank rating usually means the account keeps a stronger average balance.

Low 5 usually points to a low five-figure average balance, often around $10,000 depending on the bank. This step explains how banks look at account age, average balance, deposits, and clean activity before the business starts asking for stronger credit.

Approval timing note

A stronger bank rating can change how the business looks before a lender reviews it.

Bank rating is about balance behavior and account history. Members should not rush into credit applications before the banking relationship looks stable.

Keep money moving through the business account.Avoid low balances immediately before applying.Build at least 90 days of clean banking behavior when possible.Track your balance, account age, deposits, and clean activity on this page before applying.
