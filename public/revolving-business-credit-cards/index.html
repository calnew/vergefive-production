Credit card timing note

Revolving credit should come after the business can support the application.

Business credit cards can be valuable, but applying too early can create unnecessary denials and inquiries. The file should show identity, banking, and starter credit strength first.

Review readiness before applying.Many business credit cards may still check the owner's personal credit or require a personal guarantee.Choose cards that match current profile strength.Keep utilization controlled after approval.Do not stack applications without a clear reason.
