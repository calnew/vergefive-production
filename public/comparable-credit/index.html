Why this matters

Bigger approvals often depend on what the business has already handled responsibly.

This step explains comparable credit: lenders often want to see that the business has already handled similar accounts before approving larger requests.

Approval leverage note

Comparable credit helps a lender understand what the business has already handled.

The stronger the existing business credit references, the easier it is to support larger approvals. Thin files need starter accounts before asking for bigger limits.

Document trade accounts and limits already approved.Avoid applying for accounts far beyond current profile strength.Use comparable accounts to choose the next credit tier.Let new accounts report before moving too aggressively.
