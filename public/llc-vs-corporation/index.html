Why this matters

The entity filing becomes the legal anchor for the business credit profile.

This step helps the member choose the legal structure that will appear on state records, banking records, tax records, and future credit applications.

Entity choice guide

LLC vs Corporation: choose the structure before you file.

LLC

Often simpler to operate, with flexible ownership and pass-through taxation by default. Many small businesses choose this when they want legal separation without corporate formalities.

Corporation

More formal structure with shareholders, directors, officers, bylaws, and stock. This can fit companies planning outside investors, shares, or a more traditional corporate structure.

Caveats

Entity choice affects taxes, ownership, annual reports, registered agent requirements, and how records must be maintained. Get tax or legal guidance if ownership, investors, licensing, or liability exposure is complicated.
