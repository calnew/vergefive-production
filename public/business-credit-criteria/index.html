Why this matters

The criteria prevent members from applying before the business profile is ready.

This step is the readiness checkpoint. If any core item is missing or mismatched, stop here and fix it before applying for vendors, cards, or funding.

Readiness gate

The 12-point criteria are the stop sign before unnecessary denials.

This checkpoint protects members from applying before the profile is prepared. If several criteria are incomplete, the next move is fixing the foundation, not submitting more applications.

Complete identity, legal, banking, and proof items first.Do not apply if the business record is inconsistent.Use the criteria as a go or no-go review.Save the completed checklist as readiness proof.

12-point readiness review

Confirm every signal before applications

If one or two items need cleanup, fix them before moving into vendor credit, cards, or funding. A denial is usually more expensive than waiting a few days to correct the profile.

1

Legal business name

Use the exact legal name everywhere, including applications, bank records, website, and directories.

2

EIN confirmation

Confirm the EIN letter and tax records match the legal business name.

3

Active state entity

The Secretary of State or filing office should show the company active and in good standing where required.

4

Valid business address

Use a real commercial-style address, not a mailbox-only or post office style location that can trigger flags.

5

Business phone number

Use a reputable business phone service, not a mobile number sitting in a mobile database.

6

Business 411 listing

Put the business phone number in Business 411 so lenders can verify it as a commercial signal.

7

Professional website

The website should be live, credible, and consistent with the services listed on applications.

8

Domain email

Use a business email on the company domain instead of Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, or another personal email.

9

Business bank account

The account should be opened under the exact legal name and used for business activity only.

10

Bank rating and activity

Review average balance, account age, deposits, and clean activity before stronger applications.

11

Licenses and records

Any license, registration, permit, or professional record should match the business identity.

12

Bureau visibility

Check NAV.com, bureau resources, or monitoring tools for visibility, missing data, and mismatches.
