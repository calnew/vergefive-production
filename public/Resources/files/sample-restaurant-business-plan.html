1. Executive Summary

Harbor & Main Bistro LLC is a planned fast-casual restaurant serving breakfast, lunch, light dinner items, coffee, and grab-and-go meals to local residents, workers, tourists, and small business owners in the Daytona Beach area. The company will focus on consistent food quality, efficient service, clean branding, and a neighborhood-friendly customer experience.

The business will operate from a leased commercial space with approximately 1,600 to 2,000 square feet, seating for 40 to 55 guests, online ordering, catering trays, and local delivery through approved delivery partners.

The owner is seeking to finalize lease terms, build out the kitchen and front-of-house area, purchase equipment, open vendor accounts, hire the first team, and maintain enough working capital for the first six months of operations.

Funding request example: $185,000 total startup funding, including $75,000 owner contribution and $110,000 in bank or alternative financing.

Mission

To provide reliable, fresh, reasonably priced meals in a clean, welcoming environment for customers who want better everyday food without long wait times.

Objectives

  • Open the restaurant within 120 days after funding and lease signing.
  • Reach monthly sales of $62,000 by month six.
  • Maintain food cost near 31 percent and labor cost near 28 percent of sales.
  • Build business credit relationships with food, janitorial, packaging, and equipment vendors.

3. Market and Customer Profile

The target market includes local residents, nearby employees, contractors, tourists, students, and small business owners who need convenient meals. The restaurant will compete with national chains, independent cafes, diners, and delivery-only food concepts.

Before signing a lease, the owner should verify traffic count, parking, neighboring businesses, zoning, nearby competition, delivery radius, and permitted kitchen use.

4. Products and Services

  • Breakfast bowls, sandwiches, pastries, coffee, tea, and juice.
  • Lunch bowls, salads, wraps, soups, and hot plate specials.
  • Grab-and-go meals for nearby offices and delivery customers.
  • Catering trays and boxed lunch packages for business customers.
  • Seasonal promotions built around local events and weekend traffic.

The menu will be designed around ingredients that can be used across multiple items to control waste, simplify training, and protect margins.

6. Marketing and Sales Plan

  • Launch a professional website with menu, ordering link, catering inquiry form, address, phone, and business email.
  • Build Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, and local directory listings.
  • Use a pre-opening email list, grand opening week, and local chamber introductions.
  • Offer office catering samples to local businesses within a 3-mile radius.
  • Track repeat customers through the POS and promote weekly specials through email and social media.

Marketing will be measured by website visits, online orders, review volume, email signups, catering leads, customer count, and average ticket.

7. Management and Ownership

Sample owner Jordan Hayes will manage daily operations, supplier relationships, banking, payroll, quality control, and customer experience. Before opening, the owner should secure professional support from a CPA or bookkeeper, insurance agent, attorney or legal document provider, payroll provider, and food safety consultant if required.

The owner should also create written policies for cash handling, refunds, scheduling, inventory, food safety, customer complaints, and vendor purchasing.
