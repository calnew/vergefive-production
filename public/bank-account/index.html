Business bank account walkthroughWatch lesson

Why this matters

Banking history shows stability, cash flow, and whether the business can handle credit.

This step helps the member open and use the business bank account under the exact legal name so deposits, balances, and banking history support future applications.

Choose your banking relationship

Start with a local or community bank when possible.

A smaller relationship bank can be more useful than a big national bank when the business needs a real banker, local decision-making, and future loan support.

Relationship bank finder

Choose your state to build a bank shortlist.

Use this section to find FDIC-insured community banks, regional banks, and local credit unions with business banking support. Then compare them using the questions below.

Best first choice

A local community bank or strong regional bank where you can speak with a business banker and build a relationship.

Best for relationship lending and future loan conversations.

Use big banks carefully

Large national banks can be useful for convenience, but underwriting can be less personal and more automated.

Good for branch access, not always best for early lending.

Ask before opening

Ask whether they offer business lines of credit, secured loans, SBA lending, credit cards, and a named business banker.

Do not choose based on a free checking promo alone.

Banking foundation warning

The bank account is not just for deposits. It becomes a relationship and stability signal.

Lenders look for a real operating account with consistent activity, clean handling, and enough balance history to support future applications. Whenever possible, start with a local community bank or regional bank where you can build a relationship with a business banker.

Open the account in the exact legal business name with the same address, phone, and EIN from prior lessons.Choose relationship banking over convenience alone; a local banker may understand the business better when it is time to discuss credit.Local and community banks can sometimes move faster on small-business loans because the decision process may be closer to the customer.Avoid overdrafts, returned items, rapid account changes, and low-balance history before major credit applications.
