What Is PAYDEX and Why Does It Matter?

PAYDEX is tied to payment behavior reported to Dun & Bradstreet.

Research noteBusiness Credit2026-05-09

Search intent

Searchers ask what PAYDEX means and how to improve it.

The basic idea

PAYDEX is a Dun & Bradstreet score associated with how a business pays its bills. In general, paying on time is important, and paying early can be stronger in models that reward early payment behavior.

You need reported data

A business cannot build a strong score without reportable payment experiences. That is why vendor selection matters. If a vendor does not report, the payment may help operations but may not help the bureau profile.

Do not obsess over one score

PAYDEX matters, but it is not the only business credit signal. Lenders may also look at banking, revenue, time in business, personal credit, industry risk, and other bureau data.

Quick readiness check

  • D&B profile checked
  • Reporting vendors selected
  • Invoices paid early
  • Score monitored
