The Hidden Gatekeepers framework

Automated approval systems judge the business before a person ever looks at it.

Verge Five starts with the scan and Module 1 because modern financing reviews look for matching business identifiers first. The business name, address, phone, website, email, filings, banking, and timing signals need to line up before vendor, card, or funding applications go out.
