Pre-module concept

Understand NAP before you start Module 1.

NAP means business Name, Address, and Phone. It is not an extra module. It is the identity rule that sits underneath the whole buildout.

NAP information overviewWatch before Module 1

Business identity rule

Name, address, and phone must match everywhere.

The exact legal business name, commercial address, and business phone number should match across the website, Business 411, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, data aggregators, and major directories. If one record shows a mobile number, old address, mailbox-style address, or different business name, the company can look inconsistent.

Use one exact legal business name everywhere. Use the same commercial address format on every listing. Use the business phone number, not a personal mobile number. Update website, directory, and map listings before applying for vendor credit.
This is awareness, not a requirement to subscribe to a listing service.
A business can build credit without buying a NAP service. The important part is understanding that public consistency matters before applications.
