Why this matters

Members need to know where vendor and lender data may appear before they apply.

This step helps the member understand which business credit bureaus may hold company data and why missing or mismatched bureau records can slow approvals.

Mini glossary

What these bureau names mean

D&BDun & Bradstreet, the bureau connected to D-U-N-S numbers and PAYDEX-style payment history.
Experian BusinessA business credit bureau that may collect company, trade, and risk data.
Equifax BusinessA commercial bureau that may show business identity, risk, and payment information.
CreditsafeA business credit report provider used to review visibility, risk, and company profile data.

Check what is reporting

Business bureau and monitoring resources

Use these resources as checkpoints. The goal is not to obsess over one score. The goal is to see whether the company is visible, whether the records match, and whether any trade activity is starting to appear.

Creditsafe

Business credit report resource used to review company visibility, risk indicators, payment data, and business profile information.

Use it to see whether the company is visible and whether details look consistent.
Open Creditsafe

Equifax Business

Business credit bureau resource for understanding business reports, risk signals, and data that may be used in commercial decisions.

Use it to understand bureau reporting and identify records that may need correction.
Open Equifax Business

NAV.com

NAV is a monitoring resource where business owners can review business credit information, track profile activity, and see selected bureau data depending on the plan and available records.

Use it before and after building trade accounts so you can monitor what appears over time.
Open NAV.com

Bureau visibility note

Business credit bureaus do not all see the same information at the same time.

Members need to understand where data may appear, which vendors report, and why business identity consistency matters before applications start. Check whether the company is visible, review what is reporting, and save screenshots or notes so you can compare the profile again after vendor accounts, cards, or funding activity begin to report.
