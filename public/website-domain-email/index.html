Why this matters

Vendors look for a business that appears real, reachable, and consistent online.

Your website and business email work together. A real domain, professional website, and domain-based email address help vendors and lenders see that the company is legitimate, reachable, and consistent before applications begin.

Website approval standard

Your website has to look like a real business, not a placeholder.

If you build it yourself, make sure it has a professional design, clear service pages, a business contact page, domain-based email, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service. A domain email uses your website name, like info@yourcompany.com, instead of Gmail or Yahoo. A missing business email is a major red flag because it can make the company look unfinished or not legitimate. Use an email on your domain, such as firstname.lastname@yourdomain.com, info@yourdomain.com, or billing@yourdomain.com.

Use the same business name, phone, address, and email from your records.Add Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, About, Contact, and service pages.Create business email on the same domain as the website. The provider where you buy the domain or website, such as GoDaddy, Wix, Webflow, Google Workspace, or Microsoft 365, should be able to help set up domain email.Make the site mobile-friendly and remove coming-soon or unfinished sections.If you cannot make it look professional, use a professional website service.
