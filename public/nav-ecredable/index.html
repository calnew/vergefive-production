Fast track strategy

How Nav Boost and eCredable can work together.

Use both as support tools after the business foundation is clean.

Nav Boost

Nav helps the member monitor the business credit profile, understand bureau visibility, and depending on the plan, may add a reporting subscription tradeline that supports profile activity.

eCredable Business Lift

eCredable may help report eligible business bills or vendor payments, which can add more payment history when the business file is thin and needs additional reportable activity.

Together

Nav gives visibility and monitoring while eCredable may add eligible payment data. Used together, they can help a clean business profile show more activity sooner, but they still do not replace EIN, phone, address, website, banking, and vendor readiness.

Start only after the business name, address, phone, website, email, EIN, and bank account match.Use Nav to monitor what is showing and eCredable to explore eligible bill or vendor payment reporting.Confirm exactly which bureaus each service reports to before paying for a plan.Save screenshots, plan details, and reporting proof for the business records.

Fast track caution

Optional reporting tools can help visibility, but they do not replace proper setup.

Nav, eCredable, and similar tools may help members monitor or add certain payment history, but the foundation still has to be accurate.

Use optional tools after identity and banking are clean.Confirm what each service actually reports.Do not confuse monitoring with guaranteed approval.Save screenshots or reports that show profile progress.
