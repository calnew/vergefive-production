Starter Vendor Credit

Net 30 accounts and starter vendor strategy. Net 30 means the business buys now and pays the invoice in about 30 days.

Section 1Starter vendor credit readiness
Net 30 walkthroughWatch lesson

Why this matters

Starter tradelines are useful only when the business profile is ready to be reviewed.

This step helps the member use Net 30 accounts only after the profile is ready, so starter vendor applications build history instead of creating avoidable denials.

Vendor readiness matcher

Vendor requirements can change. This matcher is a readiness guide, not a guarantee of approval. Always verify current requirements before applying.

Vendor strategy note

Net 30 accounts should be used to build reporting history, not just to buy supplies.

Starter tradelines work best when the business is already set up cleanly and purchases are paid on time. The goal is controlled reporting history.

Start with vendors that match the business stage.Buy only what the business can pay back cleanly.Pay early or on time every cycle.Wait for reporting before stacking too many accounts.
