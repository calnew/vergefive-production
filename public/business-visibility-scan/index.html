Direct answer

A business visibility scan shows what the public can find before you apply.

The scan is a baseline. It helps identify whether the business name, phone, address, website, and public-facing profile look complete enough to keep building.

What it checks

The scan focuses on the business identifiers that should be visible and consistent.

A visibility scan is not an approval engine and does not guarantee financing. It is a practical check that helps the owner see whether the basic public signals are missing, weak, or inconsistent before applications.

Common mistake

Owners often assume the business is easy to verify because it exists. The scan asks a different question: can the outside world find the same business identifiers that will appear on applications?

Next step
