Why this matters

The EIN connects the legal entity to banking, tax, and vendor records.

This step helps the member get the EIN under the exact legal business name so tax records, bank records, and applications all match.

Get or verify your EIN with the IRS

EIN stands for Employer Identification Number.

It is the federal tax ID number the IRS assigns to the business. Apply directly through the IRS. Do not pay a third-party site just to get an EIN unless you are intentionally using a filing service.

Open official IRS EIN application

Make the EIN record match the business you are building.

Do not rush through the EIN application. The EIN record becomes part of the business identity. If the IRS record, Secretary of State record, bank account, website, phone, address, and vendor applications do not match, the business can look inconsistent before credit is ever reviewed.

Legal business nameUse the exact legal name from the approved Secretary of State record.
Entity typeChoose the structure that matches the state filing, such as LLC, corporation, partnership, or sole proprietor.
Responsible partyUse the person who owns or controls the business and its assets. Their SSN or ITIN may be used to request the EIN, but that is different from the business EIN.
Business addressUse the address you plan to use consistently for banking, vendors, directories, payment processors, and applications.
Business phone numberUse the business phone number when possible, not a random personal number, so the business identity stays consistent.
Principal business activityChoose the category that best describes what the business actually does. Do not choose a random category just to finish the application.
Legal name vs DBAUse the legal name for the EIN. Only enter a trade name or DBA if the business actually uses one.

Best order

  1. Finish business phone and address setup.
  2. File or verify the Secretary of State record.
  3. Copy the exact approved legal name.
  4. Apply for the EIN through the IRS.
  5. Save the EIN confirmation letter.
  6. Use the same name, address, phone, entity type, and EIN details everywhere.

IRS record warning

Do not let the EIN become the first place the business name gets inconsistent.

The EIN confirmation letter should match the legal entity name exactly. Banks, vendors, payment processors, and bureaus can compare this record against state filings and applications.

Do not apply for an EIN until the legal business name is final.Use the exact legal name from the approved state filing.Save the EIN confirmation letter before opening bank accounts.Do not use trade names or abbreviations unless they are officially registered.If the EIN record is wrong, correct it before building credit history.
