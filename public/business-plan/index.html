Why this matters

A plan gives the business a credible operating story when banks or lenders ask for documentation.

This step helps the member document what the business does, how it makes money, and why future credit requests make sense.

Documentation note

A business plan gives the company a credible story when a bank asks what it does.

A simple, professional plan can help explain revenue, operations, ownership, use of funds, and realistic projections. Review the sample PDF first, then build your own plan so the numbers, market, and operating details match your actual business.

Keep the plan clear and realistic.Match the business description to the website and applications.Include revenue model, market, and use of funds.Save a PDF copy with your business records.
