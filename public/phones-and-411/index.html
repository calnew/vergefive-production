Step 1A
Business phone setupWatch this video first, then choose and activate your business phone number.

Why this matters

Your business phone number is one of the first identity signals vendors check.

This step helps vendors and lenders confirm that the business phone number is a real commercial signal, not a mobile number or throwaway line that can flag the profile.

Business 411 directory warning

Use a real business phone number that can be listed in commercial business databases.

Business 411 is a commercial directory listing that helps banks, vendors, and lenders verify the company phone number under the business name.

For business credit, the phone number is not just for receiving calls. Lenders and vendors may compare the number against business phone directories, commercial databases, and carrier information. The goal is for the number to look like a real business line connected to the exact legal business name.

Use a reputable VoIP or business phone provider that can support a proper business listing. TurnCom360, RingCentral, and Grasshopper are examples of providers that fit this path better than low-cost, fly-by-night phone services.A cheap phone line may save a few dollars, but it may not support the kind of business identity signals you need. You get what you pay for when lenders are checking consistency.Do not use a mobile number, even if the mobile carrier calls it a business line. Mobile numbers can show in mobile databases, and that can flag the company during lender or vendor checks.If you already have a business number, add it to the business 411 directory and keep the name, phone, address, website, and email consistent everywhere.

Step 1B
Add the number to the Business 411 directoryDo this after your business phone number is active.

Required next step

The phone number is not finished until it is listed in Business 411.

After you get the business phone number, add that exact number under the exact legal business name in the Business 411 directory. This helps vendors, banks, and lenders verify that the company can be found through commercial directory records.

Use the same business name, phone number, and address everywhere.Follow the walkthrough video and save proof of the listing.Do not move to the next identity item until the number and listing match.
