In this lesson, we are going to talk about your business bank rating.

This is one of the quiet signals that can make a big difference before you apply for stronger business credit.

When a bank or lender looks at your company, they are not only looking at whether you have an account. They are looking for stability.

That means your average balance, how long the account has been open, whether deposits are coming in consistently, and whether the account is being handled cleanly.

The goal is to build a banking relationship that makes the business look dependable.

A Low 5 bank rating is often used as a practical checkpoint, because it suggests the account has enough balance strength to support larger requests.

But do not rush this step.

If the account is brand new, balances are too low, or there are overdrafts and returned items, the business may not look ready yet.

Before you move forward, review your average balance, set a target balance plan, keep money moving through the account, and avoid NSF activity.
