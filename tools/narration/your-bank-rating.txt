In this lesson, you are going to track your banking foundation instead of guessing. The bank rating worksheet helps you look at the account like a lender might look at it. You want to know the account age, average balance, daily balance habits, deposits, and whether the account has stayed clean. This is not about pretending the business is ready. It is about knowing where the business stands right now. Record your current balance range, set a target, and watch how the account behaves over time. Save monthly statements as proof. If the account is too new or the balance is too low, the next move is not more applications. The next move is strengthening the bank relationship before moving forward.
