In this lesson, you are using the official state filing office as the source of truth. Every state has its own Secretary of State, corporation commission, or business filing office. That official record is what banks, vendors, payment processors, and lenders may compare against your applications. Do not rely on memory or old paperwork if the state record says something different. Use the dropdown on this page to find the correct filing website for your state. Search the business name, confirm the status, verify the address and registered agent, and save a copy of the state record. If the state record is wrong, fix it before moving into banking or credit applications. The goal is to make sure the legal record and every future business credit signal match.
