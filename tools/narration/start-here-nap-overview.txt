NAP stands for Name, Address, and Phone. Before you start building business credit, understand that this is your company identity fingerprint.

Vendors, lenders, banks, directories, and search platforms look for consistency. The same legal business name, the same commercial address, and the same business phone number should appear across your website, Business four one one, Google Business Profile, maps, and major directories.

This does not mean you must buy a listing service to build business credit. It means you need to be aware of how important consistency is. If one place shows a mobile number, another shows a different address, and another shows a slightly different company name, the business can look unverified.
