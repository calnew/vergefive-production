In this lesson, we are looking at business credit bureaus and why visibility matters. Business credit data can appear with different bureaus at different times. Dun and Bradstreet, Experian Business, Equifax Business, and other providers may not all show the same information. That is why consistency matters so much. The legal name, address, phone number, website, E I N, bank records, and vendor applications all need to line up. Do not assume that every vendor reports to every bureau. Also do not assume that a missing or thin profile means something is wrong. The goal is to understand where your profile may appear, correct mismatches early, and choose vendors or accounts that support the kind of reporting history you are trying to build.
