In this lesson, we are looking at certificate-of-deposit secured business loans. This can be a lower-risk way to build a banking credit relationship when it is used correctly. The idea is simple. The business places money in a certificate of deposit or secured account, and the bank lends against that deposit. Because the loan is secured, the bank has less risk. But the business still has to make payments exactly as agreed. This strategy is not free money. It is a way to document clean repayment history with a financial institution. Before using this path, confirm the terms, understand how the deposit is held, ask whether the relationship can help your business credit file, and save all loan documents and payoff proof.
