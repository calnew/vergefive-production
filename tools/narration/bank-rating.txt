In this lesson, we are going to talk about your business bank rating. This is one of the quiet signals that can make a big difference before you apply for stronger business credit. When a bank or lender looks at your company, they are not only looking at whether you have an account. They are looking for stability. That means your average balance, how long the account has been open, regular deposits, and clean account activity. The goal is to build a banking relationship that makes the business look dependable. A low five bank rating is often used as a practical checkpoint because it suggests the account has enough balance strength to support larger requests. Before you move forward, review your average balance, set a target balance plan, keep money moving, and avoid N S F activity.
