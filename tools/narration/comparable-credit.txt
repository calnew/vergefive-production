In this lesson, we are talking about comparable credit. Comparable credit means the business has already handled accounts that support the next request. If a lender sees that the company has managed small vendor accounts responsibly, it may be easier to understand why the business is ready for a larger account. If the file is thin, asking for too much too soon can create unnecessary denials. Start with the accounts that fit your profile stage. Pay them cleanly. Let them report. Then use that history to decide what the next tier should be. The goal is not to chase the biggest approval. The goal is to build a business credit file that makes the next approval make sense.
