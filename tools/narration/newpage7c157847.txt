In this lesson, we are choosing the legal structure for the business. This matters because the entity filing becomes the legal anchor for your business credit profile. An L L C is often simpler to operate, with flexible ownership and pass-through taxation by default. A corporation is more formal, with shareholders, directors, officers, bylaws, and stock. Neither choice is automatically better for every business. The right choice depends on ownership, taxes, liability, investors, licensing, and long-term plans. Before you file, make sure the legal name, address, registered agent, and ownership information are correct. If the setup is complicated, get tax or legal guidance before moving forward. Once the entity is filed, save the stamped formation proof and use the exact legal name on every future record.
