In this lesson, the twelve-point criteria work like a readiness gate. This is the checkpoint before applying for vendor accounts, cards, or funding. If the business name, phone, address, website, E I N, banking, and state records do not match, the profile may look risky or incomplete. The purpose of the checklist is to stop unnecessary denials before they happen. Do not treat it like busy work. Treat it like a pre-application review. If several items are incomplete, the answer is not to keep applying. The answer is to fix the foundation first. Once the criteria are complete, save the progress report and use it as proof that the business profile is ready for the next step.
