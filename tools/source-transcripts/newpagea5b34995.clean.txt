In this lesson, the 12 point criteria work like a readiness gate before vendor accounts, cards, or funding.

Do not treat this like busy work. Treat it like a pre application review.

The first point is legal business name consistency.

The second is E I N confirmation.

Third, the entity must be active with the state.

Fourth, the business address should be a valid commercial address, not a mailbox-only location.

Fifth, the business phone should be a real business number.

Sixth, that phone number should be listed in Business four one one.

Seventh, the website should be live and professional.

Eighth, the business should use domain email, not a personal email address.

Ninth, the business bank account should be open in the exact legal name.

Tenth, the bank rating and account activity should show stability.

Eleventh, any required license, registration, or professional record should match.

Twelfth, bureau and public records should be checked for visibility and mismatches.

If several items are incomplete, the answer is not more applications. The answer is to fix the foundation first, save proof, and then move forward.
