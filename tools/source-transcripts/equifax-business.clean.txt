In this lesson, we are looking at business credit bureaus and why visibility matters before you apply.

Business credit data can appear with different bureaus at different times. Dun and Bradstreet, Experian Business, Equifax Business, Creditsafe, and other providers may not all show the same information.

That is why consistency matters so much. The legal name, address, phone number, website, E I N, bank records, and vendor applications all need to line up.

You also need to monitor what is actually reporting. NAV dot com is one place business owners can use to review business credit information, monitor profile activity, and see certain data connected to major business credit bureaus, depending on the plan and available records.

Use NAV, Equifax Business, Creditsafe, and bureau resources as checkpoints. Check whether the company is visible. Look for mismatched names, addresses, phone numbers, missing trade accounts, old records, or data that does not belong to your company.

Do not assume every vendor reports to every bureau. Do not assume every bureau updates on the same timeline. And do not assume a thin file means something is wrong.

The goal is to see what exists, correct mismatches early, save proof, and choose accounts that support the reporting history you are trying to build.
