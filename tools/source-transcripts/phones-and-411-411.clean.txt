In an ever-connected world, making your business accessible is key. Here's how you can add your business telephone number to the Business four one one directory. First, navigate to list yourself dot net. Then go to the first tab, Business Listing. Hit the More Info button. Next, select the Business button and fill out your business information. When completed, click the Call Me button. The system will dial your business number. Then enter the four-digit P I N provided on the screen, and a confirmation message will pop up, stating your number is now listed in the Business four one one directory. Side note, if your number has an auto-attendant or virtual answering system, you need to choose the Call Me, but I have an auto-attendant button. Make sure to enter your extension number in the box provided. This streamlined process not only ensures your business is reachable, but also boosts your professional digital footprint.
