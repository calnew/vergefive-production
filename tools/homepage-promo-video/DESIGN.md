## Style Prompt
Premium business credit education platform promo. Clean dark navy canvas, white cards, teal-blue accents, restrained gold warnings, crisp product UI screenshots, direct-response pacing for business owners who have been burned by bad credit advice. Visuals should feel credible, protective, and practical, not flashy or guru-like.

## Colors
- Deep navy background: #071733
- Platform navy: #0b3558
- Verge blue: #1769aa
- Teal accent: #20c6d4
- Success green: #16825f
- Warning amber: #b88a2c
- White surface: #ffffff
- Muted text: #d8e9f7

## Typography
Use Inter or system sans-serif. Heavy, tight headlines; readable body copy; all UI labels clean and restrained.

## What NOT to Do
- No hype graphics, cash explosions, or fake guarantee language.
- No childish colors or loud gradients.
- No tiny unreadable UI screenshots.
- No cluttered overlays covering the platform visuals.
- Do not promise approvals; emphasize readiness, timing, and avoiding wasted applications.
